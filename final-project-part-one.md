# Outline 
With a focus on 2024, this project focuses on identifying high need counties in New York specific to health conditions and nutrition. The goal is to provide New York legislators with actionable information on which counties require high financial resources. This is a resource legislators can utilize to advocate for bills and more funding of research supported interventions. Interventions will focus on how to intervene for low income seniors and those with social isolation or living alone. 

Effect of Isolation on Health and Finances -- Problem Definition
> This is the reality of seniors ages 50 and older living in New York. These conditions effect expected life length, quality of life, and costs this state tax payer money that could be spent elsewhere. The audience is New York legislators so they understand which counties have higher counts of each condition and are higher need.

> The problem will first be defined for an audience of New York legislators. Brief statistics of large, bolded numbers will be provided to introduce and emphasize the effect of living status and isolation on senior health, specifically mental health rates. This is an introduction and discussion of why this topic is important. Because this focus is on New York and their legislators, New York seniors' death rates, health effects, and financial costs of isolation, mental health, and lower income will be provided. The data identifies the need, while the research tells which interventions should be utilized. 

County Analysis of Lower Median Income and Highest Population of seniors 50+ in each county out of total population: Identifying High Need Counties
> This will identify which counties have the highest concentration of the population we want to support. Counties with higher need will be those with a higher percent of elderly population compared to the average.  For median income, counties with higher need those with lower-than-average median


High Need County Analysis of Mental Health and Living Status 
> By understanding the correlation of mental health and living status for high need counties, we can understand if higher percent of seniors living alone is associated with higher mental health cases in a county. The goal is to highlight which counties are highest need. 

Effect by Race and Gender
> Maps will be created to compare and contrast between White and Black, as well as female and male New Yorkers. This will help understand if there is a race or gender population that requires greater focus.
> The goal is to identify if different races of genders have different needs. This allows legislators to specifically tailor condition and population specific financial and health resources where they are needed most.

Ranked County Table:
> For the sake of actionability, a simple table ranked from higher need to lower need counties will be created. This will focus on income, living status, and population rates. Legislators will be able to identify which counties have higher need with a one minute glance.
