# User research 

## Target audience
> Representative individuals of the target audience, New York legislators are previous of future policy makers or policy professionals. The criteria were professionals who understand what legislators care about and the questions they would have in order to take legal and policy action to make the lives of seniors better. I found seasoned and previous professionals who have experience with policy making and understand what it takes to go from recommendation to action. I also found fellow Heinz classmates who I know want to work in policy in the future. Since they understand stakeholders and policy and how to influence them, I interviewed them together and received actionable critiques. 


## Interview script
> The goal was to understand how this data can not only be informational but also actionable. Due to time, one or two questions were prioritized. The question was asked for each interview was "as a legislator, based on this story, what questions or information would you require in order to take steps regarding legislation?" A question specifically asked in interview one was "how many recommendations should this project have for legislators?"

## Interview findings
> Detail the findings from your interviews.  Do not include PII.  Capture specific insights where possible.
Interview One, Previous Policy Professional:
> Specific resources that are recommended for legislators would need to be identified. Some examples are resources for transportation,  senior centers, and healthy food. Overall, the feedback was to define the needs and resources required. The costs associated with each county should be defined.
> The focus should be that legislators should know what the next logical step, such as programs, is instead of a general recommendation. An important recommendation was also making sure to acknowledge what the other considerations are that are not available in data, as well as what more research should be conducted. Policy recommendations and programming. What funding sources for counties for programs. The project should not underestimate how many recommendations to tell legislators and 5 to 10 recommendations of actionable steps could be optimal. Furthermore, different counties may have different needs so understanding what county specific stakeholders or legislators think, and counties' specific needs would tailor recommendations. Because more research can be required for detailed recommendations, a possible recommendation could also be to ask for more resources to research. A possible research request could be for a resources per county/capita analysis. The goal would be to understand what resources are already in each county so new or expanded resources are taking in to account the resources available. A topic that was repeated in two of the five interviews was "what resources are already available" so research on resources already in each county would be helpful. 

Interview Two, Previous Policy Professional:
The first critique was to begin with a anecdotal story in order to personalize the data. The second was to make sure the separate indicators somehow overlap to identify high need counties, instead of just stand-alone indicators of need. The goal is this citique was to make it easier for legislators to know which counties, on all the different indicators, are higher need, instead of single indicators. Another topic of focus was to identify which resources are already in each county in order to further establish and narrow need. 

Interviews Three to Five, Future Professionals [Conducted at Once]
The main critiques were to identify how much the recommended resource allocation would cost each county and New York state as a whole. In this regard, the cost effectiveness of these recommendations would help legislators prefer allocating resources to these above other policies. Another critique was to include and overlap other indicators to establish the need of counties. An indicator that was recommended was HDI or human development indicators, such as spend on healthcare.

# Identified changes for Part III
> I plan to somehow overlap indicators so counties that are high need on multiple indicators can be prioritized. I also plan on attempting to identify data for other indicators that can be utilized to establish need. I also plan to conduct more research on the cost of social isolation and of policies to combat social isolation. I also plan to clearly identify the needs and recommended resources, as well as identifying what kind of future research should be conducted to make this information actionable for legislators. Future research can be on county specific resources already available, as well as county specific budget constraints. Overall, I will follow recommended structure of anecdotal, overlapped indicators, specific actionable recommendations. 
> Because county specific recommendations are not possible with the time constraint, need specific recommendations would allow legislators and stakeholders to identify recommendations that would best address their county specific need.
